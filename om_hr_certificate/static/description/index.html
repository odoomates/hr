Features

What it does

✓  Employees ask for a certificate in My HR > My Certificates, with who it is for and why. Their manager, then the HR managers, approve it along an approval flow; a refusal carries its reason.
✓  Once approved, the certificate is printed from its template, numbered, and the employee is told and downloads it. The text is kept as printed.
✓  Salary Certificate, Employment Certificate, Experience Certificate and No Objection Certificate to start with; HR managers edit them, or add their own, in Employees > Configuration > Employee > Certificate Templates. Employees Can Request decides which ones employees ask for.
✓  HR also prints any certificate from the Print menu of an employee; a Certificates smart button on the employee lists what was issued.
✓  Employees > Certificates: To Approve, All Requests and Issued Certificates.
✓  A template can carry a verification QR code, and can file each certificate as a document of the employee, e.g. an NOC valid for 30 days, when Document Expiry is installed.
✓  Employees see their own requests and certificates only; HR managers cancel an issued certificate.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Adjust the templates, their text, signatory and signature in Employees > Configuration > Employee > Certificate Templates.
✓  Change who approves in Employees > Configuration > Requests > Approval Flows, flow Certificates: by default the manager of the employee, then the HR managers; the employee does not approve his own request.
Use

How it works

1.  The employee creates a request in My HR > My Certificates, chooses the certificate and the addressee, and submits it.
2.  The approvers find it in Employees > Certificates > To Approve, or in My HR > To Approve, and approve or refuse it.
3.  Once approved, the employee opens the request and clicks Download.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_base, om_print_template, om_hr_approval.
