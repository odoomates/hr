Features

What it does

✓  A Shifts card on My HR opens /my/hr/shifts.
✓  My Upcoming Shifts: the published shifts of the employee from today on.
✓  Open Shifts: the published open shifts of their company the employee may claim; Claim sends the claim for approval.
✓  Swap a Shift: one of their shifts against a published shift of a colleague of the same department, with a word for the colleague; the request goes to the colleague.
✓  Swaps Asked of You: the colleague asked accepts or declines the swap.
✓  Days I Cannot Work: add or remove the days the employee is not available.
✓  The last 20 requests made by or asked of the employee, with whom they wait for and their status.
✓  Each employee sees only their own records: changing an id in the address gives a page not found, not someone else's record.
Use

How it works

1.  The employee opens My HR > Shifts.
2.  They claim an open shift, ask a colleague for a swap, answer the swaps asked of them, or add the days they cannot work.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_portal, om_hr_shift. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
