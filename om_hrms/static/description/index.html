Why a bundle

One download, every app ready on the shelf

Downloading this bundle brings every Odoo Mates HR app into your Odoo at once, in versions made and tested together. Nothing to hunt for, no missing dependency, no app to download again the day you need it: the next app, and the bridge linking it to what you already use, are already there, waiting for you to install them.

This bundle is a delivery, not something to install. Odoo Mates HR is built the Odoo way: modular. Every app installs on its own, and the links between two apps are small bridges that install themselves once both are there.

How to use it

Add it to Odoo, then pick what you need

  1. 1Download — Download this bundle from the App Store: it holds all the Odoo Mates HR modules.
  2. 2Add to Odoo — Unzip it into the addons folder of your Odoo 20 (every om_* folder side by side) and restart Odoo. On Odoo.sh, add them to your repository.
  3. 3Update the list — In Apps, turn on the developer mode and click Update Apps List.
  4. 4Pick your apps — Search for Odoo Mates and install the apps you need today, one by one: Payroll, Loans, Documents, Appraisals... Each brings only what it needs.
  5. 5Grow — Come back to Apps whenever a new need shows up, and install the next one. The bridges with the apps you already use install themselves.
Do not click Install on “Odoo Mates HR” itself unless you really want every app. Installing the bundle installs all 58 apps and bridges at once, with the Odoo apps they need (Time Off, Attendances, Recruitment, Maintenance, Surveys, Dashboards...). Most companies are better served by picking the apps one by one.

What's inside

24 apps to pick from

Foundation

What the other apps build on

  • HR Base om_hr_base
    Shared base of the Odoo Mates HR modules: the My HR menu and the employee category of the contract
  • Approvals om_approval
    Approval flows for any app: a button waits for its approval before it runs, e.g. confirming a sale order above an amount
    Not with Enterprise Approvals
  • HR Approvals om_hr_approval
    Approvals by the manager, the department manager, the coach or HR, and HR requests of the employees
    Not with Enterprise Approvals

Pay

From the contract to the bank

  • Odoo 20 HR Payroll om_hr_payroll
    Payroll for Odoo 20 Community: salary structures and rules, payslips, payslip batches and reports
    Not with Enterprise Payroll
  • Payroll Adjustments om_hr_payroll_adjustment
    Payroll bonuses, commissions, fines and deductions: once, monthly or until a total, taken by the payslips
    Not with Enterprise Approvals, Payroll
  • HR Loans om_hr_loan
    Employee loans and salary advances: request, approval flow, monthly installments, repayment
    Not with Enterprise Approvals
  • File Layouts om_file_layout
    Design the CSV, fixed width or Excel files you send out of Odoo and export them from the Action menu
  • HR Compensation om_hr_compensation
    Salary grades and bands, compa-ratio, and merit cycles with department budgets turning the raises into contract versions

Time

Who works when, and on time

  • Shifts om_hr_shift
    Shift planning and rosters: shift templates, roster board, rotations, open shifts, shift swaps, rest checks
    Not with Enterprise Approvals

    Late arrivals and early departures against the working schedule: grace minutes, penalty matrix, warnings, excuses
  • Overtime Pre-Approval om_hr_overtime_request
    Authorise overtime in advance; overtime beyond it waits for the manager to accept or reject
    Not with Enterprise Approvals

Joining

Talent

Grow and listen to your people

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Odoo Mates HR works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. 21 of the 62 modules work next to any Enterprise app. A few apps define the same records as an Enterprise app and cannot be installed together with it: our Payroll with Enterprise Payroll, Appraisals with Enterprise Appraisals, Sign with Enterprise Sign, Approvals with Enterprise Approvals, and the apps built on them. Each app above says so: on Enterprise, use one or the other.
