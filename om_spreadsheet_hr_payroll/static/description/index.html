Features

What it does

✓  Dashboards > Human Resources > Payroll:
  • gross and net pay, employees paid, average gross and overtime paid over the Period;
  • gross and net pay month by month;
  • gross pay by department and by job position, the pay by category, and the deductions by rule;
  • the top earners of the period;
  • Period and Department filters; the companies selected in the company switcher.
✓  It reads the Payroll Analysis of the confirmed payslips, the refunds counted negative, and works without Accounting.
Setup

Configuration

The dashboard is for the payroll officers (Payroll: Officer access).

Use

How it works

Confirm the payslips, then open Dashboards, then Payroll in the Human Resources section, and set the Period and Department filters.

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Payroll app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

spreadsheet_dashboard, om_hr_payroll. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
