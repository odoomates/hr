Features

What it does

  • ✓  New approvers for the steps of any flow: Manager of the Employee, Manager of the Department, Coach of the Employee and HR Responsible of the Contract. The employee is the one the record is about, else the employee of the requester, e.g. the salesperson confirming a sale order.
  • ✓  A step by field can also point to an employee, e.g. employee_id.parent_id.
  • ✓  In a flow where the requester does not approve his own steps, the employee the request is for does not approve it either, e.g. when HR asks on his behalf, unless nobody else can.
  • ✓  HR requests: employees ask HR for a document, a change of their details or anything else, in My HR > My Requests. The request goes to their manager, then to the HR officers.
  • ✓  Request categories with a description shown to the employee: Document, Change of My Details and Other to start with.
  • ✓  Employees > Requests > To Approve and All Requests; My HR > To Approve lists everything waiting for your approval, from every app.
  • ✓  A step waiting too long is escalated to the managers of its approvers, by default.
  • ✓  With Time Off, validated time off names the substitute of the approver for its dates (HR Approvals: Time Off).
Setup

Configuration

  • ✓  HR managers set who approves the HR documents in Employees > Configuration > Requests > Approval Flows, and the categories in Request Categories.
Use

How it works

  • 1.  The employee creates a request in My HR > My Requests, chooses its category, writes the details and submits it.
  • 2.  The approvers approve or refuse it from Employees > Requests > To Approve or My HR > To Approve.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_approval, om_hr_base. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
