Features

What it does

✓  A Policies card on /my/hr.
✓  /my/hr/policies: the policies asked to the employee, those to answer first, with their due dates.
✓  /my/hr/policies/<id>: the text or the PDF of the version; I Have Read and Agree answers it, or Sign opens the signing page of the policies to sign.
✓  The emails of the policies lead the portal employees to their page.
✓  Each employee reaches only the policies asked to them: any other address answers Not Found.
Use

How it works

Grant the portal to the employee (Employees > the employee > Grant Portal Access); they find their policies in My Account > My HR > Policies, and the documents to sign in My Account > Signatures.

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Sign app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_portal, om_hr_sign. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
