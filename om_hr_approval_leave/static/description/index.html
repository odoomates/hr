Features

What it does

  • ✓  When the time off of an employee who approves is validated, a delegation of their approvals is created for its dates: to the substitute set in their preferences, else to their manager.
  • ✓  The substitute is asked the approvals waiting for the employee meanwhile; the employee still can decide.
  • ✓  The delegation follows the time off: changed dates move it, a refused or cancelled time off removes it.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_approval, hr_holidays. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
