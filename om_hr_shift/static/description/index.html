Features

What it does

✓  Shift templates (Shifts > Configuration > Shift Templates): the hours of a kind of shift, an unpaid break and when it starts, a short name (e.g. M for Morning) and a color. A shift ending at or before its start ends the next day, e.g. a night shift 22:00-06:00. Optional: the departments and job positions working it, the time type of its work entries (e.g. night work paid at its own rate) and an allowance per shift, paid with *Shift Allowances in Payroll*. Morning, Evening and Night templates are provided.
✓  Rotations (Shifts > Configuration > Shift Rotations): a cycle of shifts and days off, e.g. *2 Mornings, 2 Nights, 2 Off* (provided). Shifts > Apply a Rotation lays it over a period for some employees, each one day further in the cycle when staggered.
✓  Roster (Shifts > Roster): the planning Gantt chart by employee, by day, week or month, with the open shifts on top. Drag to plan and move shifts, copy the period before, publish and send the schedule. Drafts are hatched, conflicts in red, and the time off and unavailability of the employees greyed.
✓  Conflicts: time off or a public holiday, unavailability, overlapping shifts, less rest between the shifts of two days than the minimum, more hours in a day or a week than the maximum. The planner sees them on the shift and on the Gantt chart before publishing.
✓  Published shifts are the working schedule: on its first published shift, an employee is rostered: a new version of the contract from that day, on a working schedule of their own, the days before keeping their schedule. Each published shift is written on it as dated lines; changed or sent back to draft, the schedule follows. Slots planned for a document (an order, a task...) are work within the schedule, not shifts.
✓  Rostering (button on the employee form): start rostering an employee from a date, or stop it and go back to a fixed working schedule; the shifts from that date on are then removed.
✓  Open shifts: a shift with no employee, optionally for a department. Employees see the upcoming ones in My HR > Open Shifts and claim one, if the department and the job positions of the shift fit.
✓  Shift swaps: an employee offers a shift of theirs for a published shift of a colleague; the colleague accepts or declines. Claims and accepted swaps go along the approval flow (the manager by default); approved, the shifts change hands. Planners follow all requests in Shifts > Shift Requests.
✓  Unavailability: the employees tell the days they cannot work (My HR > My Unavailability); they are time off of their resource, shown on the roster and as conflicts. The planners see them all in Shifts > Unavailability.
✓  Employees see their published shifts in My HR > My Shifts.
✓  Shifts list, calendar and pivot views.
✓  Upgrading from the former Shifts: the shifts, templates, rotations, shift requests (with their approvals) and unavailability of the former versions are moved to the planning when the module is updated, with the same moments, states and links: published shifts keep their lines of the working schedule and their payslip, requests waiting for an approval keep waiting for it. The minimum rest and the maximum hours per week of the companies become their planning rules.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Access rights, application *Shifts*: Planner plans and publishes the shifts and plans with the *Planning* app (the HR officers are planners too); Administrator also sets the shift templates and the rotations.
✓  Shifts > Configuration > Settings: the planning rules per company: minimum rest between the shifts of two days, maximum hours per day and per week; 0 turns a check off.
✓  Employees > Configuration > Requests > Approval Flows > *Shift Requests*: the steps approving the claims and the swaps, one Manager step by default.
Use

How it works

1.  Set the shift templates and, if you work in cycles, the rotations.
2.  Open Shifts > Roster and plan the shifts of the employees, or apply a rotation.
3.  Check the conflicts, then publish: the working schedule of the employees follows the shifts.
4.  Leave a shift without an employee to offer it as an open shift.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.
