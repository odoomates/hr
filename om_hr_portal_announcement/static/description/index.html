Features

What it does

  • ✓  An Announcements card on /my/hr.
  • ✓  /my/hr/announcements: the published announcements addressed to the employee, the important and the unread ones first, with their status (Unread, To Acknowledge, Acknowledged).
  • ✓  /my/hr/announcements/<id>: the message and its attachments; opening it marks it read.
  • ✓  I Have Read and Understood: acknowledges the announcement when it asks for it.
  • ✓  Each employee sees only the announcements addressed to them: any other address answers Not Found.
Use

How it works

Grant the portal to the employee (Employees > the employee > Grant Portal Access); they find their announcements in My Account > My HR > Announcements.

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_portal, om_hr_announcement. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
