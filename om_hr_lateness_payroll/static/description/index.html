Features

What it does

✓  Payroll > Adjustments > Lateness Penalties: for a month, and for some or all employees, the latenesses are counted again, then their penalties become one *Lateness* adjustment per employee (type ADJ_LATE, a deduction, its own payslip line), taken by the payslip of the month.
✓  Or tick *Lateness Penalties Every Month* in the Attendances settings: it runs on the 1st for the month before.
✓  The penalties: a fixed amount, a percentage of the daily wage, or the minutes at the hourly wage, from the wage rates of the payroll (days per month of the company, hours per day of the schedule), within the monthly cap of the policy. Warnings cost nothing.
✓  A penalty of minutes is left out for a contract paid on attendances: the Attendance Adjustment rule takes the hours missed off already.
✓  Computed again, or after an excuse, the adjustment follows the latenesses; once a confirmed payslip took it, it no longer changes and the latenesses it penalised are no longer excused.
✓  Each lateness shows its penalty amount and its adjustment; the adjustment lists the latenesses it penalises.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Attendances > Configuration > Settings > *Lateness Penalties Every Month*, per company.
✓  Payroll > Configuration > Adjustment Types > *Lateness*: a deduction with no approval, whatever is still due taken by the final settlement of an employee leaving.
Use

How it works

1.  Early in the month, open Payroll > Adjustments > Lateness Penalties, choose the month before (the default) and click *Compute*.
2.  Check the adjustments, then compute the payslips of the month.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals and Payroll apps: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_payroll, om_hr_lateness, om_hr_payroll_adjustment. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
