HR Gratuity

End of service gratuity computed on the exit, from a scheme of days of pay per year of service

Bands of yearsPart kept by reasonMinimum serviceCap in months

Part of Odoo 20 HRMS - Odoo Mates · Odoo 20 Community and Enterprise

Days of pay by band of years

e.g. 21 days for each of the first five years, 30 for each year after

Reduced by reason of leaving

e.g. a resignation keeps part of it, by years of service; others keep all

Computed on the exit

From the start of service to the last working day, at the wage of that day

Features

What it does

✓  Gratuity schemes: the days of pay per year of service in each band of years, e.g. 21 days for each of the first five years and 30 days for each year after; the part kept by reason of leaving and years of service, e.g. a third for a resignation between 2 and 5 years; a minimum service below which no gratuity is due; and a cap in months of wage.
✓  The Gratuity tab of an exit (HR officers): the scheme, by default the first one of the company, and the date the service started, by default the first contract date of the employee.
✓  Compute Gratuity: the years of service up to the last working day, the monthly wage of the contract on that day, the days of pay earned, the gratuity, and how it was reached, e.g. "5.00 years × 21 days, 3.50 years × 30 days".
✓  The days of pay are paid at the monthly wage over the Days in a Month of Pay of the scheme. The part kept is that of the first line matching the reason and the years of service; the other reasons keep all of it.
✓  Registering the departure computes the gratuity when it was not computed yet.
✓  With HR Exit in Payroll, the final settlement payslip pays the gratuity.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Employees > Configuration > Requests > Gratuity Schemes (HR administrators): the name, Days in a Month of Pay (e.g. 30, or 26 working days), Minimum Service (Years), Cap (Months of Wage), the bands of Days of Pay per Year of Service (from, to, days; a band with no end covers every year beyond), the lines of Part Kept by Reason of Leaving (reasons, from, to, part kept in %), the company and notes. 0 means no minimum or no cap. No scheme comes with the module.
Use

How it works

1.  Describe the gratuity of the company in a scheme.
2.  On an approved exit, open the Gratuity tab, check the scheme and the start of service, and click Compute Gratuity.
3.  Register the departure; with HR Exit in Payroll, create the final settlement.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.
