Features

What it does

  • ✓  Pulse surveys on the Surveys application: a Pulse Survey is installed (the eNPS question from 0 to 10, three statements from 1 to 5 and an open question); change it, or pick any other survey.
  • ✓  Truly anonymous: launching a pulse sends each employee their own one-time link by email. The link opens a ballot that knows the department of the employee, never the employee: the invitations hold who was asked, the ballots hold the answers, and nothing links one to the other. The ballots are created in a random order and only the hash of each link is stored; the invitation emails are deleted once sent. Not even an administrator can tell who answered what, so there are no reminders per person either.
  • ✓  Results by department, from five answers: the eNPS, the average of each statement and the comments, sorted, for everyone and for each department. The departments with fewer than five answers are pooled and shown together from five answers; else they are left out of every result, Everyone included, since comparing Everyone with the departments shown would tell what they answered.
  • ✓  eNPS: the share of promoters (9 and 10) less the share of detractors (0 to 6), from -100 to 100. Each pulse keeps its participation and eNPS when it closes, for the trend over time (graph view).
  • ✓  Repeating pulses: every N weeks, the next pulse is created when one closes and launched on its start date; a daily job closes the pulses whose deadline passed.
Use

How it works

  • 1.  Employees > Pulse Surveys (HR officers): create a pulse, choose the departments (or all), its deadline and how often it repeats, and Launch.
  • 2.  The employees answer from the email.
  • 3.  Read the Results tab while it runs; the pulse closes on its own after the deadline.
