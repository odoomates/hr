Payroll for Odoo 20 Community

Pay your employees from Odoo 20 Community Edition: salary structures and rules, allowances and deductions kept as data, rates and tax tables that change with the date, payslips computed from the contract and the time off, in batches, with the payslip printed or emailed.

Payslip

From the contract to the net, rule by rule

Pick the employee and the period: the employment version, the structure and the worked days are filled in, and the payslip is computed rule by rule — basic, allowances, deductions, gross and net — each line showing the quantity, the rate and the amount it came from.

✓  Salary Computation, and the same figures added up by category.
✓  Draft, confirmed and cancelled: a confirmed payslip is a document, and only a payroll manager can reopen it.
✓  Refund a payslip as a credit note when something has to be undone.
✓  Print the payslip or its details, and send it to the employee by email.

A computed payslip: basic, allowances, deductions, gross and net.

Worked days

The time off is counted for you

The days and the hours the employee was due to work come from the working schedule, and the time off of the period is taken out of them and listed on its own line, at the rate its type is paid.

✓  A time off paid in full, in half or not at all, as its type says in the Time Off application.
✓  The Unpaid Time Off rule takes the unpaid part off the salary by itself.
✓  Other inputs for what changes every month: overtime, a bonus, a one-off deduction.
✓  A version amended in the middle of a period is used as amended: one payslip, one version.

Worked days and inputs on the payslip.

Components

What you pay besides the wage, as data

Housing, transport, a meal allowance, the repayment of an advance — describe each one once as a salary component, then give it its amount on the employment version of each employee. No code to write for a new allowance.

✓  A component belongs to a category, so the Allowances and Deductions rules pick it up on their own.
✓  A default amount on the component, overridden per employee where it differs.
✓  A component belongs to one company, or is shared by all of them.

Salary components: allowances and deductions described once.

Rules

Structures and rules for the pay of your country

A salary structure is a set of rules, and a structure can inherit from another one. Each rule says when it applies and how much it is worth.

✓  A condition that is always true, a range on an amount, or a line of Python.
✓  An amount that is fixed, a percentage of something else, or computed in Python.
✓  Child rules, categories, a sequence, and a switch to keep a rule off the printed payslip.
✓  Contribution registers to group what is owed to a body, with a report of the lines of each one.

The salary rules of a structure.

Rates and tables

A rate that changes should not mean changing a rule

Rule parameters hold the rates and the amounts that the law changes every year; salary brackets hold the tax tables. Both are dated, so a payslip of last year is still computed with the figures of last year.

✓  A rule reads a rate with parameters.PENSION_RATE and a table with brackets.INCOME_TAX.
✓  Marginal brackets, where each slice is taxed at the rate of its band, as most income taxes work.
✓  Excess brackets, a fixed amount plus a rate on what goes over the floor of the band.
✓  Flat brackets, the rate of the band applied to the whole amount.
✓  A new table each year, and several tables in parallel for the statuses that are taxed differently.
Batches

The whole payroll of the month, in one go

Create a batch for the period, generate the payslips of the employees you choose, compute them all and confirm them together.

✓  The payslips of a batch are listed with their status, and each one stays a payslip of its own.
✓  Send every payslip of the batch to its employee by email in one action.
✓  Monthly Batch: the batch of the month created for you on the day you choose, its payslips in draft to check.
✓  An employee without a running contract in the period is reported instead of being skipped in silence.

Payslip batches, one per period.

Reporting

What the payroll costs, from every angle

A pivot and a graph over the payslip lines: the net, the gross or any category, by month, by department, by employee or by rule.

✓  The confirmed payslips of the period, or the drafts as well.
✓  Exported to a spreadsheet from the pivot.
✓  Add the Payroll Accounting module to post the payslips to the books and pay them.

Payroll analysis: the net by department.

New in 2.4

Checked before it is paid

✓  Variance check: Mark as Done of a batch first lists the payslips whose gross or net pay changes more than 10% from last month, with the rule that moved most.
✓  Annual statement: the pay of the payroll year, rule by rule and month by month, printed by HR and downloaded by each employee.
✓  Contributions stopping at a yearly ceiling in one line, with ytd.capped.
✓  A refund takes back the lines the payslip paid, as they were paid.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Payroll app: both define the payslips and the salary rules. Use one or the other.
