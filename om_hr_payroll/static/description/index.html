Payroll for Odoo 13 Community

Pay your employees from Odoo 13 Community Edition: salary structures and rules, payslips computed from the contract and the time off, in batches, with the payslip printed.

Payslip

From the contract to the net, rule by rule

Pick the employee and the period: the contract, the structure and the worked days are filled in, and the payslip is computed rule by rule — basic, allowances, deductions, gross and net.

✓  Salary Computation, and the same figures added up by category.
✓  Draft, Waiting, Done and Rejected.
✓  Refund a payslip when something has to be undone.
✓  Print the PaySlip or the PaySlip Details report.

A computed payslip.

Worked days

The time off is counted for you

The worked days of the period are taken from the working schedule, and the time off of the employee is listed on its own lines, ready for the salary rules.

✓  The days and the hours come from the working schedule of the employee.
✓  Each type of time off of the period gets its own line.
✓  Other inputs for what changes every month: overtime, a bonus, a one-off deduction.
Rules

Structures and rules for the pay of your country

A salary structure is a set of rules, and a structure can inherit from another one. Each rule says when it applies and how much it is worth.

✓  A condition that is always true, a range on an amount, or a line of Python.
✓  An amount that is fixed, a percentage of something else, or computed in Python.
✓  Child rules, categories, a sequence, and a switch to keep a rule off the printed payslip.
✓  Contribution registers to group what is owed to a body, with a report of the lines of each one.
Batches

The whole payroll of the month, in one go

Create a batch for the period, generate the payslips of the employees you choose, compute them and confirm them together.

✓  The payslips of a batch are listed with their status, and each one stays a payslip of its own.
✓  Payslip batches are for payroll managers.
Accounting

Post the payslips to the books

Add Payroll Accounting to post a journal entry for each confirmed payslip, from the accounts of the salary rules. Install it from Payroll > Configuration > Settings.

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 13 Community and Odoo 13 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Payroll app: both define the payslips and the salary rules. Use one or the other.
