Features

What it does

✓  Register the Payments, an option of Export Bank File, with the Bank Journal (the first bank journal of the company by default) and the Payment Date.
✓  On Download, the payslips of the file still to pay are paid in Accounting, as Pay Salaries does: one payment per employee, matched with the net salary of the journal entry of the payslips.
✓  The payslips already paid, the refunds and the payslips without a posted entry are left out; the payment status of each payslip follows.
Setup

Configuration

The option is shown to the users with the Accounting rights of Invoicing (Billing) or more. The net salary rule needs a credit account that allows reconciliation, as for Pay Salaries.

Use

How it works

1.  Click Export Bank File on a confirmed payslip batch or payslip.
2.  Tick Register the Payments, check the Bank Journal and the Payment Date.
3.  Click Download: the file is exported and the payments are registered.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Payroll app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_payroll_export, om_hr_payroll_account. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
