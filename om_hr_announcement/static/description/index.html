Features

What it does

  • ✓  Announcements with a title, a formatted message and attachments, in Employees > Announcements.
  • ✓  Audience: the whole company, departments (their sub-departments included), job positions, employee tags or chosen employees; the number of employees reached is shown before publishing.
  • ✓  Shown from a date and until another: published at once or scheduled, then expired.
  • ✓  Important announcements come first and are highlighted.
  • ✓  Each employee gets the announcement in their inbox, or by email as they prefer.
  • ✓  Employees read their announcements in My HR > Announcements and from the megaphone of the top bar, which counts what waits for them; opening an announcement marks it read.
  • ✓  Requires Acknowledgement: each employee confirms they have read and understood it.
  • ✓  The authors follow the read and acknowledged rates and the list of the employees still pending; Remind Pending notifies them again, or automatically after a number of days.
  • ✓  Employees > Announcements > Announcement Report: read and acknowledged by department.
Setup

Configuration

  • ✓  HR officers publish to anyone.
  • ✓  A department manager publishes to the departments they manage, and their sub-departments, only.
  • ✓  A cron job publishes the scheduled announcements, expires the ended ones and sends the automatic reminders, every hour.
Use

How it works

  • 1.  Employees > Announcements > New: write the title and the message, choose the audience, the dates, and whether it requires an acknowledgement.
  • 2.  Publish: the employees reached are notified, now or on the day it shows from.
  • 3.  Follow the Read and Acknowledged buttons of the announcement; Remind Pending when needed.
