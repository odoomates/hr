Features

What it does

  • ✓  Start Pre-boarding on an employee whose contract starts soon: the new hire gets a portal access, if they have no user yet, and a welcome email.
  • ✓  The steps of the company, asked to all the new hires or to some departments, job positions or tags:
    • Personal Details: private address, phone and email, birth date and place, nationality, gender, marital status, spouse and children;
    • Emergency Contact;
    • Bank Account, for the salary: created on the work contact of the employee;
    • ID Numbers: identification, passport, visa and work permit numbers;
    • Read: a page to read, e.g. the first day;
    • Task: something to do, confirmed by the new hire.
  • ✓  HR reviews the details given: the values the employee has now next to those given, then Apply writes them on the employee, or Send Back asks the new hire to correct them with a note. A field left blank keeps what the employee has.
  • ✓  The welcome page of the new hire shows their progress, their steps, the first day information and their buddy (the manager by default).
  • ✓  HR follows the new hires in Employees > Pre-boarding: progress, what to review, and an activity for each answer to review.
  • ✓  Optionally, an onboarding plan of the employees is launched when the pre-boarding starts, e.g. to prepare the laptop.
  • ✓  The pre-boarding closes on the first day; the employee keeps their portal access, and HR may invite them as a user of the backend.
  • ✓  With the bridges: documents to upload (HR Pre-boarding: Documents), policies to agree to and documents to sign (HR Pre-boarding: Sign), and a start on its own from accepted job offers (HR Pre-boarding: Job Offers).
Setup

Configuration

  • ✓  Employees > Configuration > Pre-boarding Steps: the steps, ready-made ones included.
  • ✓  Employees > Configuration > Settings > Pre-boarding: the first day information and the onboarding plan.
Use

How it works

  • 1.  On the employee form, Start Pre-boarding.
  • 2.  The new hire follows the link of the welcome email, then My Account > My HR > Welcome.
  • 3.  Review their answers from Employees > Pre-boarding.
