Features

What it does

✓  Final Settlement (payroll officers), on an approved or closed exit: creates the payslip "Final Settlement of" the employee, from the first day of the month of the last working day to the last working day, with the contract, the structure, the worked days and the inputs of that period, and computes it. The Final Settlement button at the top of the exit opens it.
✓  The final settlement pays up to the last working day, even before the departure ends the contract.
✓  Unused time off: the days left on the allocations of the time types paid out at the exit, on the last working day, are paid at the daily wage, the monthly wage over the days per month of the payroll settings. The exit shows the days and the amount (HR officers). Salary rule Unused Time Off (code LEAVE_ENCASH, category Allowance).
✓  End of service gratuity: with HR Gratuity, the gratuity of the exit is paid by the salary rule End of Service Gratuity (code GRATUITY, category Allowance).
✓  Loans: with HR Loans in Payroll, the final settlement deducts every loan installment still due, whatever its month.
✓  The amounts are read again from the exit each time the payslip is computed.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Employees > Configuration > Time Management > Time Types, Payroll tab: tick Paid Out at the Exit on the time off to pay out.
✓  Payroll > Configuration > Settings, Daily Wage: the days per month the monthly wage is divided by, for the unused time off.
✓  The rules Unused Time Off and End of Service Gratuity are added to the structure "Base for new structures". The structures that already exist are left as they are: add both rules to them in Payroll > Configuration > Salary Structures, with the Partial Period rule of the payroll to prorate the last salary; without it the whole wage of the last month is paid.
Use

How it works

1.  Approve the exit (HR Exit), and compute its gratuity with HR Gratuity.
2.  Click Final Settlement: the payslip is created and computed.
3.  Check it, and confirm it as any payslip.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals and Payroll apps: both define the same records. Use one or the other.
