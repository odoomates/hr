Features

What it does

  • ✓  Dashboards > Human Resources > Workforce:
    • headcount, joiners, leavers, turnover and average service (years) over the Period, and the headcount, joiners and leavers month by month;
    • the employees by department, job position, nationality and type;
    • the contracts ending in the period, archived employees included;
    • Period and Department filters; the figures cover the companies selected in the company switcher.
  • ✓  The OM.HR formulas, usable in any spreadsheet: Departments are names separated by commas, e.g. ODOO.FILTER.VALUE("Department"); empty for all. An empty date means today.
    • OM.HR.HEADCOUNT(date, departments): the employees under contract on a date, the archived ones included for the past;
    • OM.HR.JOINERS(date_from, date_to, departments) and OM.HR.LEAVERS(date_from, date_to, departments): the employees whose contract started or ended between the two dates;
    • OM.HR.TENURE(date, departments): the average service in years, counted from the first contract of each employee.
Setup

Configuration

The dashboard and the formulas are for the HR officers (Employees: Officer access); other users get an access error from the formulas.

Use

How it works

Open Dashboards, then Workforce in the Human Resources section, and set the Period and Department filters. The figures read the contract dates of the employees: fill in the contract start and end dates on the employees.

Editions
