===============================
Talent and Compliance Dashboard
===============================

Part of `Odoo Mates HR <https://github.com/odoomates/hr>`_, the HR suite of Odoo Mates for Odoo 20
Community.

The Talent and Compliance dashboard for the HR officers: the appraisals, the
employee documents to renew, the exits and the assets with the employees.

Features
========

* Dashboards > Human Resources > Talent and Compliance:

  * appraisals to do and done, the ratings of the appraisals done, and the
    appraisals by status;
  * the documents of the employees expiring and expired, to renew by type, and
    the list of the documents to renew;
  * the exits in progress (to approve or approved), and the exits by reason;
  * the assets assigned to the employees;
  * Period and Department filters.

Configuration
=============

The dashboard is for the HR officers (Employees: Officer access).

Usage
=====

Open Dashboards, then Talent and Compliance in the Human Resources section, and
set the Period and Department filters.

Dependencies
============

* ``spreadsheet_dashboard`` (Dashboards), ``om_hr_appraisal`` (HR Appraisals),
  ``om_hr_document`` (HR Documents), ``om_hr_exit`` (HR Exit) and
  ``om_hr_asset`` (HR Assets). Installs by itself with them.
