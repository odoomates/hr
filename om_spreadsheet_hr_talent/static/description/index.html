Features

What it does

  • ✓  Dashboards > Human Resources > Talent and Compliance:
    • appraisals to do and done, the ratings of the appraisals done, and the appraisals by status;
    • the documents of the employees expiring and expired, to renew by type, and the list of the documents to renew;
    • the exits in progress (to approve or approved), and the exits by reason;
    • the assets assigned to the employees;
    • Period and Department filters.
Setup

Configuration

The dashboard is for the HR officers (Employees: Officer access).

Use

How it works

Open Dashboards, then Talent and Compliance in the Human Resources section, and set the Period and Department filters.

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Appraisals and Approvals apps: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

spreadsheet_dashboard, om_hr_appraisal, om_hr_document, om_hr_exit, om_hr_asset. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
