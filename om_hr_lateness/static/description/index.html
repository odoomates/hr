Features

What it does

✓  Each working day with attendances, the first check-in is compared with the start of the working schedule and the last check-out with its end. Time off and public holidays are left out, so a half day off or a holiday never counts as late; a night shift ends the next morning; flexible schedules are skipped. The schedules of the shift rosters are read the same way.
✓  Recorded as soon as the attendance is, and again every night for the last week, to follow the time off and the attendances changed since.
✓  Lateness policy: grace minutes on arrival and on departure, whether early departures count, a monthly cap in days of wage, and a matrix of penalties: a band of minutes, from an occurrence in the month on, gives a warning, a fixed amount, a percentage of the daily wage, or the minutes at the hourly wage times a factor. Late arrivals and early departures are numbered apart; the numbering starts again each month. A *Standard* policy is provided: beyond 10 minutes, the time late or left early at the hourly wage.
✓  A warning messages the employee.
✓  The attendance managers excuse a lateness, with a reason: it is no longer counted, and the numbering of the month follows. *Count Again* undoes it.
✓  Attendances > Management > Lateness for the managers, with a pivot by month; My HR > My Lateness for the employees. With Payroll Adjustments, *HR Lateness Payroll* takes the penalties off the payslips.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Attendances > Configuration > Lateness Policies: the grace minutes and the penalties.
✓  Attendances > Configuration > Settings > Lateness Policy: the policy of the company; the employee form sets a policy of its own. Without one, the first policy of the company, or shared by all companies, applies.
Use

How it works

1.  Employees check in and out as usual.
2.  The managers review Attendances > Management > Lateness and excuse what should not count.
3.  The employees follow theirs in My HR > My Lateness.
