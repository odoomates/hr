Features

What it does

✓  New Offer on the applicant: job position, department, monthly wage, start date, employee type, working schedule, end of probation, other benefits and the date the offer is valid until; prefilled from the applicant (proposed or expected salary, availability).
✓  Approval before sending, along an approval flow you change in Approvals (HR managers by default; e.g. the department manager first, or only above a wage).
✓  Send for Signature issues the Offer Letter (an editable print template with the terms) and sends it through Sign: the person sending signs first for the company, then the candidate countersigns from a link sent by email, without an account.
✓  The offer expires with its signature request on its Valid Until date; a recruiter can withdraw a sent offer.
✓  Accepted, the applicant moves to the hired stage with the salary and the availability of the offer; Create Employee fills the contract of the new employee: wage, start date, employee type, working schedule, end of probation, job and department.
✓  Declined offers keep the reason given by the candidate; New Offer from This One starts again from a refused, declined, expired or cancelled offer.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Adapt the Offer Letter in Print Templates > Templates (its text, signers and the email code option).
✓  Change who approves the offers in Approvals > Configuration > Approval Flows > Job Offers.
Use

How it works

1.  Open an applicant, New Offer, fill the terms and Submit for Approval.
2.  Once approved, Send for Signature, then sign the letter from Sign > To Sign.
3.  Recruitment > Applications > Offers lists the offers and their status.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals and Sign apps: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

hr_recruitment, om_sign, om_approval. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
