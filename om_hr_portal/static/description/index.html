Features

What it does

  • ✓  Grant Portal Access on the employee form (HR officers) creates a portal user for the employee, with the work email (or the private email) as login, and emails them an invitation to set their password. It refuses when the employee already has a user, when the email is the login of a backend user, or when that portal user already belongs to another employee.
  • ✓  A My HR card on the portal home (/my) opens /my/hr: the photo, job and department of the employee, and a card per HR page installed.
  • ✓  My Profile (/my/hr/profile): job, department, manager, joining date, work email and phones, company. It is read only; the employee tells HR what to correct.
  • ✓  The pages of the HR modules come from small bridge modules that install by themselves with the HR module they serve: payslips, time off, loans, certificates, documents, assets, appraisals, resignation, pay adjustments and shifts.
  • ✓  Each page shows only what belongs to the employee logged in: changing an id in the address gives a page not found, not someone else's record.
Setup

Configuration

Only HR officers (group hr.group_hr_user) see and use Grant Portal Access. An employee already linked to a user reaches /my/hr with that user.

Use

How it works

  • 1.  Set the work email of the employee.
  • 2.  On the employee form, click Grant Portal Access and confirm.
  • 3.  The employee follows the link of the invitation email, sets a password and finds My HR on the portal.
Editions
