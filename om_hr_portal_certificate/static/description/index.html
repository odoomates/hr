Features

What it does

✓  A Certificates card on My HR opens /my/hr/certificates.
✓  Ask for a Certificate: the employee chooses the certificate, gives the addressee and the purpose, and sends the request, which is submitted for approval as from the backend.
✓  The list of their requests shows the certificate, the addressee, the date asked and the status.
✓  Once the certificate is issued, the PDF button downloads it (/my/hr/certificates/<id>/pdf).
✓  Each employee sees only their own records: changing an id in the address gives a page not found, not someone else's record.
Setup

Configuration

Only the certificate templates on employees with Employees Can Request ticked are offered, those of no company or of the company of the employee (Employees > Configuration > Certificate Templates).

Use

How it works

1.  The employee opens My HR > Certificates, chooses a certificate and sends the request.
2.  Once HR has approved and issued it, they download the PDF from the same page.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_portal, om_hr_certificate. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
