Features

What it does

  • ✓  An Appraisals card on My HR opens /my/hr/appraisals: the appraisals of the employee (cancelled ones left out) with the deadline, the status and the rating, and their goals.
  • ✓  Each goal shows its progress; the employee sets a new progress from 0 to 100 and clicks Update.
  • ✓  /my/hr/appraisals/<id>: the deadline and the meeting date. While the appraisal waits for the self assessment, the employee writes it: Save keeps it, Submit to My Manager sends it on.
  • ✓  The review of the manager shows once the appraisal is done.
  • ✓  Each employee sees only their own records: changing an id in the address gives a page not found, not someone else's record.
Use

How it works

  • 1.  The employee opens My HR > Appraisals and clicks the appraisal.
  • 2.  They write their self assessment, save it as often as needed, then submit it.
  • 3.  On the list, they update the progress of their goals.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Appraisals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_portal, om_hr_appraisal. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
