Features

What it does

✓  Paid Out registers an outgoing payment to the employee on the loan account, dated the day the loan is paid out.
✓  An installment repaid outside the payroll, or a loan settled at once, registers an incoming payment from the employee on it.
✓  The partner of the payments is the work contact of the employee.
✓  The loan account is reconciled per employee, so what each employee still owes is the balance of their partner on it.
✓  The Payments button on the loan opens its payments (invoicing users); the payment shows the loan it belongs to.
✓  With the payroll accounting, the installments deducted from the payslips credit the loan account too: see HR Loans in Payroll Accounting.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Employees > Configuration > Requests > Loan Types, Accounting section (accounting administrators): the Loan Account, a receivable account, and the Payment Journal, a bank or cash journal. Both are set per company. A loan type without them is not accounted for.
Use

How it works

1.  Set the loan account and the payment journal on the loan types.
2.  Approve a loan and click Paid Out: the payment to the employee is posted.
3.  Mark an installment Repaid, or Settle the loan: the payment from the employee is posted and matched with the payout.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_loan, account. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
