Features

What it does

✓  Print templates of the employees name who signs among the employee, their manager, their coach or any employee field, e.g. a contract signed by the employee and then by the manager. The employee signs as their work contact.
✓  Request Signature on the employee form sends any PDF, the employee suggested as signer; the Signatures smart button lists the requests about the employee and those they sign.
✓  HR officers send documents for signature and read the signature requests concerning employees.
✓  Policies, e.g. the code of conduct, the IT use or an NDA: their text written in the editor or uploaded as a PDF, version after version.
✓  Each policy is answered by Read and Agree (one click, logged with the date, the user and the IP address) or by a signature through Sign (signed PDF and certificate).
✓  A policy is asked to the whole company, departments (with their sub-departments), job positions, employee tags or some employees, with a due date and reminders.
✓  Publishing a new version asks everyone again; the answers to the previous versions stay.
✓  New employees, and those moving into the audience of a policy, e.g. to a department, are asked the current version (Ask New Employees).
✓  HR follows who answered: answered / asked on the policy, the answers by department, the overdue ones, Ask Again for an employee who declined to sign or whose request expired.
✓  My HR > My Policies and My HR > My Signatures for the employees.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Employees > Policies > Policies: create the policy, its answer, its employees, then New Version to write its text or upload its PDF, and Publish.
Use

How it works

1.  The employees get an email and answer in My HR > My Policies (or on the portal with HR Portal: Policies).
2.  HR follows the answers in Employees > Policies > Policy Answers.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Sign app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_sign, om_hr_base. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
