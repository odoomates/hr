Features

What it does

✓  A salary rule, Loan Installments (code LOAN, category Deduction), with an input of the same code. It deducts the amount of the input, and is left out of the payslip when there is nothing to deduct.
✓  Computing a payslip gives it the installments of the employee's loans being repaid that are due by the end of its period and not deducted yet, installments of earlier months included, as the LOAN input. Computed again, the input follows the installments.
✓  The final settlement of an exit (HR Exit in Payroll) deducts every installment still due, whatever its month.
✓  Confirming the payslip marks its installments repaid, on the last day of the period, with the payslip as note; the loan is Repaid once all its installments are.
✓  A payslip cancelled or refunded makes its installments due again, and a repaid loan goes back to Repaying. A payslip set back to draft keeps its installments, to deduct them when confirmed again.
✓  The installments show the payslip deducting them, on the loan and in Employees > Loans > Installments.
Setup

Configuration

✓  The rule is added to the structure "Base for new structures", so the structures created from it deduct the loans. The structures that already exist are left as they are: add the Loan Installments rule to the ones that should deduct the loans, in Payroll > Configuration > Salary Structures.
Use

How it works

1.  A loan is approved and paid out in HR Loans.
2.  Compute the payslips of the month: the installments due show as the Loan Installments line.
3.  Confirm the payslips: the installments are repaid.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals and Payroll apps: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_loan, om_hr_payroll. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
