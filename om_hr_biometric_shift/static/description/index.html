How it works

A missed punch stays a missed punch

When a punch alternates (Alternate mode, or no known status), the published shift decides. Without a shift around the punch, the punches alternate as usual.

On a shift from 8:00 to 17:00Punches alternateWith the shifts
Forgets to punch at 8:00, punches at 17:0017:00 taken as a check-in17:00 is a check-out without check-in, to correct
Punches at 8:00 the next morningCloses an attendance from 17:00 to 8:00The check-in of the new day
Punches at 17:00 that dayA check-in again: every day turned aroundThe check-out

Your device is not on the list? It will be soon

We have not tested every model yet. The app speaks the standard protocols of the ZKTeco and eSSL devices, ADMS (push) and TCP on port 4370, that the models above share, and most devices of these brands with one of them work the same way.

Guide and install

Get started

Nothing to set up

Publish the shifts of your employees in the Shifts app.

1Install Biometric Attendance Machine and Shifts: this module installs by itself.
2Publish the shifts in Shifts.
3The punches of the devices follow the shifts from then on.

How to install it

1Copy the module to a folder of your own modules, e.g. /opt/odoo/custom-addons, and add that folder to addons_path in odoo.conf.
2Restart Odoo.
3Activate the developer mode, then Apps → Update Apps List.
4Clear the Apps filter, search Biometric Attendance Machine - Shifts and install it.
Do not use Apps → Import Module. It is meant for data modules without Python, and leaves these modules half loaded, ending on a missing field or view error.
Still failing? Write to odoomates@gmail.com with your Odoo version and the error.
