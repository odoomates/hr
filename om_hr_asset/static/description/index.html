Features

What it does

✓  Assign an asset to an employee, with its condition and a handover form printed for the employee to sign (Asset Handover Form, numbered ASSET/<year>/). Reassign hands it over to another employee in one step.
✓  Return it with the condition it comes back in; a damaged asset can get a repair request and a charge to the employee.
✓  Report an asset lost, with an optional charge to the employee; mark it found if it turns up.
✓  An Asset Status on every equipment (Available, Assigned, Lost), with filters to find them.
✓  The history of every asset and of every employee: who held what, when, and in which condition. Changing the employee on the equipment directly, or the departure of an employee, is kept in the history too. The employee form has an Assets tab for HR officers.
✓  Employees see the assets in their hands in My HR > My Assets.
✓  With HR Exit, an exit is cleared only once the assets of the employee are back (HR Assets in Exits). With Payroll, the charges are deducted from the payslips (HR Assets in Payroll). Menus, for the equipment managers:
✓  Employees > Assets > Assets, Available and Assignments.
✓  Employees > Configuration > Employee > Asset Categories.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Give the people who hand over the assets the Equipment Manager rights of Maintenance.
✓  Edit the text of the handover form in the print template Asset Handover Form.
Use

How it works

1.  Create the asset in Employees > Assets > Assets.
2.  Click Assign, choose the employee, the date and the condition; the handover form opens to print.
3.  When the asset comes back, click Return and give its condition; or click Report Lost.
