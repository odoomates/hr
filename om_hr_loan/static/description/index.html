Features

What it does

✓  My HR > My Loans: an employee asks for a loan or a salary advance, with the amount, the number of installments, the month of the first installment and the reason, then submits it. Compute Installments shows the schedule before submitting; submitting computes it otherwise.
✓  The installments are spread evenly, one a month from the first installment; the last one takes what the rounding leaves. A salary advance has a single installment.
✓  Submitting checks the limits of the loan type: maximum amount, maximum number of installments, minimum months of service since the first contract, an installment at most a share of the wage, and one loan of the type at a time.
✓  The request is approved along the Loans approval flow: the manager of the employee, then the HR administrators. Approvers find it under Employees > Loans > To Approve, or My HR > To Approve, and approve or refuse it with a reason. The Approvals tab of the loan keeps who decided what and when.
✓  Employees > Loans > All Loans (HR officers): Paid Out marks an approved loan as paid to the employee, and its installments become due. HR can still cancel an approved loan before it is paid out.
✓  On the Installments tab, HR postpones an installment, and the ones after it, by one month, or marks it repaid outside the payroll. Settle marks everything still due as repaid at once, e.g. when the employee repays in cash. Once every installment is repaid, the loan is Repaid.
✓  Employees > Loans > Installments (HR officers): the installments still to repay, by month.
✓  The loan shows what is repaid and what is still due. Draft and cancelled loans only can be deleted; the amount and the installments change only while the loan is a draft.
✓  Two loan types come with the module: Salary Advance (one installment, at most 50% of the wage) and Personal Loan (up to 12 installments, each at most 30% of the wage, after 6 months of service).
Setup

Configuration

✓  Employees > Configuration > Requests > Loan Types (HR administrators): the name, Salary Advance, Maximum Amount, Maximum Installments, Maximum Installment (% of Wage), Minimum Service (Months), One at a Time, the company, and a description shown to the employee asking. 0 means no limit.
✓  Employees > Configuration > Requests > Approval Flows: the Loans flow, its steps and their approvers (HR Approvals).
Use

How it works

1.  The employee creates a loan in My HR > My Loans and clicks Submit.
2.  The manager, then HR, approve it.
3.  HR hands over the money and clicks Paid Out.
4.  Each month an installment is repaid: deducted from the payslip with HR Loans in Payroll, or marked Repaid by HR. Related modules, installed on their own when their dependencies are:
5.  HR Loans in Payroll: the installments are deducted from the payslips.
6.  HR Loans in Accounting: the loans are paid out and repaid as payments on a loan account.
7.  HR Portal: Loans: employees ask for loans and follow them on the website portal.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.
