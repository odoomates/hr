Payroll Accounting for Odoo 20 Community

Post the payslips to the books and pay them: an accounting entry per payslip or one consolidated entry for a whole batch, the salaries payable reconciled with the payment of each employee, and the payment status carried back onto the payslip.

Accounts

Each salary rule knows where it is posted

Give a salary rule its debit account and its credit account — the wage expense against the salaries payable, the deductions against what is owed to the bodies that collect them — and the payslip writes the entry itself when it is confirmed.

✓  Debit account, credit account, an analytic account and a tax, per rule.
✓  A rule with no account is simply not posted: the intermediate rules stay out of the books.
✓  A salary journal on the company, on the payslip and on the batch, each falling back to the one above.
✓  The accounts are set per company, so each company of the group posts to its own chart.

The debit and credit accounts of a salary rule.

Entry

Confirm the payslip, the entry is there

The accounting tab of the payslip carries its accounting date, its salary journal, the entry it produced and where the payment of it stands.

✓  The entry is posted on the last day of the period unless you date it yourself.
✓  What is owed to the employee is written against that employee, so it can be reconciled later.
✓  A rounding difference is put on its own adjustment line rather than left to unbalance the entry.
✓  Cancelling a payslip takes its entry back with it.

The accounting side of a payslip: journal, date, entry and payment status.

Consolidated

One entry for the whole batch, without losing the employees

Tick Consolidated Entry on a payslip batch and confirming it posts a single accounting entry instead of one per payslip. The journal items of the same salary rule are added up, while what is owed to each employee keeps its own line.

✓  One line for the wages, one for the allowances, one for the deductions — instead of the same three per employee.
✓  The salaries payable of each employee stays separate, so each one is still reconciled with their own payment.
✓  The books stay readable when the payroll runs to hundreds of payslips a month.
✓  Leave it unticked and every payslip keeps an entry of its own, as before.

A payslip batch with the Consolidated Entry option.

The consolidated entry of a batch of five payslips: the rules added up, the employees kept apart.

Payment

Pay the salaries from the payslip

Register Payment on a confirmed payslip — or Register Payments on a whole batch — and the payments are created against the salaries payable of each employee and reconciled with them.

✓  The journal, the payment method and the date are asked once for the whole run.
✓  The amount to pay and what is left to pay are shown before anything is created.
✓  The payslip turns Not Paid, Partially Paid or Paid by itself, from what has been reconciled.
✓  The payments of a payslip are one click away from it.

Paying the salaries of the selected payslips.

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Payroll app: both define the payslips and the salary rules. Use one or the other.
