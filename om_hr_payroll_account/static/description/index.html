Payroll Accounting for Odoo 17 Community

Connect the payroll of Odoo 17 Community to the accounting: each confirmed payslip posts a journal entry built from the debit and credit accounts of the salary rules.

Journal entries Accounts per rule Analytic accounts Community Edition
Journal entries

Each confirmed payslip posts its entry

Confirming a payslip creates its journal entry in the salary journal and posts it: one debit and one credit line per salary rule, from the accounts set on the rule.

✓  The accounting date of the payslip, or the end of its period when it is left empty.
✓  The partner of a line comes from the contribution register of the rule.
✓  An adjustment line on the default account of the journal when the debits and the credits differ.
✓  A refunded payslip posts the opposite entry; a cancelled payslip deletes its entry.
Salary rules

The accounts on the rule

Each salary rule gets a Debit Account, a Credit Account, an Analytic Account and a Tax on an Accounting tab, used by every payslip the rule appears on.

Journals

A salary journal where you need it

The salary journal is set on the contract, on the payslip or on the payslip batch, and the payslips generated from a batch take the journal of the batch. The contract also takes an analytic account.

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 17 Community and Odoo 17 Enterprise, with Odoo 17 HR Payroll. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Payroll app.
