==============================
Biometric Attendance Dashboard
==============================

Part of `Odoo Mates HR <https://github.com/odoomates/hr>`_, the HR suite of Odoo Mates for Odoo 20
Community.

A dashboard of the biometric devices in the Dashboards app, next to the Time
Off and Attendance one. It installs by itself with the Dashboards app and
*Biometric Attendance*.

Features
========

* The punches of the period, the punches of unknown users, the punches in error
  and the attendances to review.
* Punches by day, by device, by kind of verification (finger, face, card) and
  by status; each chart opens the punches.
* The devices with their last contact, the number of punches on them and their
  firmware.
* Filtered by period (this month by default) and by device.

Usage
=====

Dashboards > Human Resources > Biometric Attendance, for the attendance
administrators.

Dependencies
============

* ``spreadsheet_dashboard``
* ``om_hr_biometric`` (Biometric Attendance)
