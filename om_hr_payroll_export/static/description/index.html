Features

What it does

✓  Export Bank File on a payslip batch or on a payslip, or Export: followed by the name of the layout in the Action menu of the payslips selected: the confirmed payslips only, without the refunds.
✓  One line per salary transfer: the net salary split over the bank accounts of the employee by their Salary Distribution (fixed amounts and percentages), the last account taking what the rounding leaves. An employee without a distribution is paid on their main bank account.
✓  The columns read payslip, employee, partner, bank, amount, net, batch, company, currency, period_from and period_to, and line('BASIC') for the total of any salary rule, as the WPS files ask.
✓  The problems before sending: an employee without a bank account, an account not trusted for payments, a payslip already in another bank file.
✓  Each payslip shows the bank files it is in (In a Bank File, Bank Files), and every file is kept in Payroll > Bank Files to download it again.
✓  Bank Transfer (CSV) to start with; copy it into the layout of your bank in Payroll > Configuration > Bank File Layouts.
Setup

Configuration

Payroll managers set up the layouts in Payroll > Configuration > Bank File Layouts: start from a copy of Bank Transfer (CSV) and change its format and its columns to the ones of your bank. The Salary Distribution and the bank accounts of the employees, and whether an account is trusted for payments, come from the employee and the contact.

Use

How it works

1.  Confirm the payslips, alone or in a batch.
2.  Click Export Bank File on the batch or on the payslip, or select payslips in their list and choose Export: Bank Transfer (CSV), or your own layout, in the Action menu.
3.  Choose the layout, check the preview and the problems, then click Download.
4.  Find the files again in Payroll > Bank Files.
Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Payroll app: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_file_layout, om_hr_payroll. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
