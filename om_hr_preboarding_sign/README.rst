=====================
HR Pre-boarding: Sign
=====================

Part of `Odoo Mates HR <https://github.com/odoomates/hr>`_, the HR suite of Odoo Mates for Odoo 20
Community.

New hires agree to the policies of the company and sign their documents as steps of their pre-boarding.

Features
========

* An Agree to the Policies step: done once no policy waits for the answer of the new hire.
* A Sign the Documents step: done once the new hire signed what was sent to them and has nothing left to sign,
  e.g. their employment contract sent through Sign.
* The steps lead the new hire to their policies and their documents to sign on the website.

Configuration
=============

Employees > Configuration > Pre-boarding Steps: add the steps Agree to the Policies and Sign the Documents.

Dependencies
============

* ``om_hr_preboarding``, ``om_hr_portal_sign``. Installs by itself with them.
