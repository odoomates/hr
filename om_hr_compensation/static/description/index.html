HR Compensation

Salary grades and bands, compa-ratio, and merit cycles with department budgets turning the raises into contract versions

Salary gradesCompa-ratioMerit cyclesBudgets

Part of Odoo 20 HRMS - Odoo Mates · Odoo 20 Community and Enterprise

Salary bands

A minimum, a midpoint and a maximum per grade, the job positions on their grade

Pay positioning

The compa-ratio of every wage and where it sits in its band

Merit cycles

Managers propose within the budget of their department, HR approves

Features

What it does

✓  Salary grades: a band of monthly wages per grade, e.g. G5 from 5,000 to 8,000, with its midpoint (halfway by default, or the market rate you set). Each job position points to its grade.
✓  Compa-ratio: the wage of the employee divided by the midpoint of the grade of their job position, and where the wage sits in the band (below it, lower, middle or upper third, above it), on the employee and in the Pay Positioning report of the current wages, by grade, department or position.
✓  Merit cycles:
  • An HR administrator creates a cycle with its effective date and a budget, a share of the wages paid today (3% by default), and generates one line per employee paid, for all the departments or those chosen. Each department gets its budget, which HR may change.
  • Open for Proposals gives each manager a to-do. In Compensation > Raises of My Team they propose a raise per employee, as a percentage or as the new wage, seeing the compa-ratio before and after; the raises of a department stay within its budget. A manager reads only the lines of their own team.
  • HR closes the proposals, approves or refuses each raise (HR may go beyond the budget), and clicks Apply: each approved raise becomes a new version of the contract of the employee from the effective date, with the new wage.
Setup

Configuration

✓  Compensation is for the HR administrators (Employees: Administrator), the users who read the wages in Odoo. The managers proposing raises read only the wages of their team, on their lines of a merit cycle.
✓  Employees > Configuration > Salary Grades (HR managers): the grades and their band.
✓  Give each job position its grade in its form.
Use

How it works

1.  Compensation > Merit Cycles: create a cycle, Generate Employees, check the budgets, then Open for Proposals.
2.  The managers propose the raises in Compensation > Raises of My Team.
3.  HR clicks Close the Proposals, approves or refuses the raises, then Apply.
