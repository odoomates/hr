Features

What it does

✓  A Pay Adjustments card on My HR opens /my/hr/adjustments: the running and done adjustments of the employee, with the type, addition or deduction, the start month, the amount each month (fixed, or a percentage of its base) and what is already done.
✓  /my/hr/adjustments/<id>: the detail of one adjustment, its recurrence, the total when it has one, and each monthly line with its amount and status.
✓  The drafts and the adjustments waiting for approval stay with the payroll and are not shown.
✓  Read only: the adjustments are made by the payroll in the backend.
✓  Each employee sees only their own records: changing an id in the address gives a page not found, not someone else's record.
Use

How it works

The employee opens My HR > Pay Adjustments on the portal and clicks an adjustment to see its months.

Editions

Community and Enterprise

Works with Odoo 20 Community and Odoo 20 Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed together with the Enterprise Approvals and Payroll apps: both define the same records. Use one or the other.

Install

Dependencies

om_hr_portal, om_hr_payroll_adjustment. It installs by itself once they are all installed.
