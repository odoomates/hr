Features

What it does

  • ✓  Each employee gets a next birthday, a next work anniversary (with the years of service, counted from the start of the contract) and a probation end, kept up to date every day by a scheduled action and whenever the dates change. The next work anniversary shows on the employee form for HR officers.
  • ✓  Ready-made Automation Rules give a to-do: to the manager on a birthday, to the manager and the HR responsible on a work anniversary, and to the manager 14 days before a probation ends (left to HR Appraisals when it is installed, as its probation reviews do it). Without a manager user, the HR responsible is reminded, else the HR managers of the company.
  • ✓  A birthday is only reminded and shown for the employees who let it be shown (Show Birthday To Employees on the employee).
  • ✓  My HR > Celebrations lists the coming birthdays and work anniversaries of the month, for every employee. The expiring contracts and work permits are reminded by Odoo itself, and the expiring documents by HR Documents.
